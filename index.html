Nova, the AI receptionist that never sleeps

Answers customer questions from a Google Sheet, qualifies people who want to book, and saves every lead for the team. It runs 24/7 and costs nothing when idle.

  1. 01A customer messages the chat, any time of day.
  2. 02The AI agent looks up the answer in the business's knowledge sheet, so it never makes up prices or hours.
  3. 03If they want to book, it collects name, contact and need, one question at a time.
  4. 04The lead is saved to a Leads sheet and the team follows up.
